CC Code

A personal desktop tool that prepares short vertical videos, uploads them as private drafts to its operator's own TikTok account, and reads that same account's follower count and video statistics into the operator's private tracking spreadsheet. Drafts are reviewed and published by hand in the TikTok app. The tool cannot post to a profile.
